**Last updated: 15 November 2026**

These terms are the rules for using Res Bot ("the bot"), a Discord bot run by the Res Hood team ("we", "us") for the Res Hood Discord server and the Res Hood Roblox game.

By using the bot, or a server where it is installed, you agree to these terms and to our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). If you do not agree, do not use the bot.

## 1. Who can use it

- You must follow [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines). This includes Discord's minimum age (13 in most countries).
- If you link a Roblox account, you must also follow the [Roblox Terms of Use](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/articles/115004647846).
- Staff-only features (moderation, dashboard, embeds, game moderation) may only be used by people the server owner has made staff.

## 2. What the bot does

Res Bot provides welcome messages, server stats, moderation tools, tickets, an invite tracker, temporary voice channels, an embed builder, Roblox account verification, leaderboards, and a connection between the Res Hood game and Discord. Features can change or be removed at any time.

## 3. Rules

When using the bot, you must not:

- Use it to harass, threaten, spam or abuse anyone.
- Try to break, overload, hack or get around its limits or permissions, including its connection to the Roblox game.
- Pretend to be someone else, or verify a Roblox account that is not yours.
- Send false reports, fake tickets, or false information to staff through the bot.
- Use the embed builder or any other feature to post content that breaks Discord's or Roblox's rules.
- Copy, resell or reuse the bot or its data without our permission.

## 4. Staff and moderation

- Moderation actions taken with the bot (bans, kicks, timeouts, warnings, game bans) are decisions made by **server staff**, not by the bot. Staff are responsible for using these tools fairly.
- If you think you were moderated unfairly, contact the staff through a ticket. We may keep moderation records even after an appeal, to protect the server.
- Breaking these terms or the server rules can lead to warnings, timeouts, bans from the Discord server, or bans from the Res Hood game.

## 5. Roblox verification and game features

- Verifying links your Discord account to your Roblox account, and may give or remove roles based on your Roblox group rank.
- You can unlink at any time with `/roblox unlink`.
- Game stats, leaderboards and tryout results come from the Res Hood game. We try to keep them accurate, but we do not guarantee they are correct.
